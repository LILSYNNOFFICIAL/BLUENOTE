BlueNote — Remember everything. Organize anything. Focus on what matters.

BlueNote is a local-first Personal Organizer and Second Brain for notes, tasks, habits, projects, calendar planning, contacts, OCR capture, and knowledge organization.

KEY CAPABILITIES:
• Local-first workspace: core notes, tasks, habits, projects, calendar data and settings work without an account.
• Brain Dump & Quick Capture: turn natural-language input into organized tasks, notes, reminders, links and other items using local parsing.
• OCR Scanner: process receipts, business cards and handwritten notes with bundled local worker, WebAssembly, and English language data; no executable OCR code is downloaded at runtime.
• PROJECTS OS: organize project documents, tasks, versions, tags and research in structured workspaces.
• Weekly Habits & Streak Analytics: track habits and review completion trends.
• Second Brain Knowledge Graph: explore relationships between notes, tasks, projects, contacts and files.
• Personal CRM: maintain contacts, links and supporting information in one workspace.
• Focus tools: use focus sessions, reminders, dashboards and energy modes without requiring an online account.

F-DROID BUILD:
The F-Droid variant is a dedicated local-only distribution flavor. It excludes the Firebase web SDK, cloud authentication, cloud workspace synchronization, third-party cloud AI providers, and online font/CDN assets. OCR worker code, WebAssembly, and English language data are bundled with the app. The app remains useful without an account or cloud service.

PRIVACY:
BlueNote is open source and local-first. Optional online integrations are separated from the core local workflow. See PRIVACY_POLICY.md and the source repository for implementation details.
