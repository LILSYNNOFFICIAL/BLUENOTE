BlueNote — Remember everything. Organize anything. Focus on what matters.

BlueNote is a complete, privacy-first Personal Organizer, Habit & Streak Analytics Tracker, Smart Note Editor, Personal CRM, Knowledge Graph, and On-Device AI Studio.

KEY CAPABILITIES:
• 100% On-Device AI (Zero API Keys Required): Natural language Brain Dump, priority synthesis, note summarization, procedural illustration/video synthesis, and ambient focus audio run locally on your device.
• Weekly Habits & 30-Day Streak Analytics: Track daily habits on a 7-day rolling checkmark grid and analyze 30-day completion rates, rolling averages, and streak momentum.
• Adaptive Energy Modes: Switch between Focused, Normal, Busy, Tired, or Vacation mode to automatically adapt your daily task load.
• Universal Capture & OCR Scanner: Extract tasks, reminders, calendar events, contacts, and shopping items from natural language or scanned receipts and business cards.
• Second Brain Knowledge Graph & Semantic Search: Explore an interactive node-and-edge network of your notes, tasks, projects, contacts, and files.
• Clean Startup & Interactive Onboarding: Starts with zero pre-filled demo clutter and guides you through a 5-step personalization tutorial.
