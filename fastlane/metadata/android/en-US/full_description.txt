BlueNote — Remember everything. Organize anything. Focus on what matters.

BlueNote is a complete, privacy-first Personal Organizer, 10 GB AI Project Operating System (PROJECTS), Quiet Predictive Pattern Engine, Habit & Streak Analytics Tracker, Smart Note Editor, Personal CRM, Knowledge Graph, and On-Device AI Studio.

KEY CAPABILITIES:
• 100% Clean Zero-Demo Startup & 5-Step Onboarding: Starts with zero pre-filled demo clutter across all modules and guides you through a 5-step personalization & theme tutorial.
• PROJECTS — 10 GB AI Project Operating System: 12-module workspace per project supporting 2 MB chunked & resumable document uploads (.txt, .md, .doc, .docx, .pdf), non-destructive version history (Original → Working → Edited → Final), side-by-side diffs, multi-document merge, standalone HTML photo album generator, and AI Sandbox Mode.
• Quiet Predictive Pattern Engine: Learns your recurring shopping, household, and routine cycles with strict Hypothesis vs. Known Fact distinction and anti-nagging safeguards.
• 100% On-Device AI (Zero API Keys Required): Natural language Brain Dump, priority synthesis, note summarization, procedural illustration/video synthesis, and ambient focus audio run locally on your device.
• Weekly Habits & 30-Day Streak Analytics: Track daily habits on a 7-day rolling checkmark grid and analyze 30-day completion rates, rolling averages, and streak momentum.
• Universal Capture & OCR Scanner: Extract tasks, reminders, calendar events, contacts, and shopping items from natural language or scanned receipts and business cards.
• Second Brain Knowledge Graph & Semantic Search: Explore an interactive node-and-edge network of your notes, tasks, projects, contacts, and files.
